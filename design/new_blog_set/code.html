How often should you replace your toothbrush?

Harbor Dental Group · 6 min read · Published by Postwright
schedule

Recommended Replacement Cycle

Every 90 days or immediately following an infectious oral illness.

Most dental professionals recommend changing your toothbrush every three months. Over time, normal wear bends the bristles outwards, significantly reducing their ability to remove plaque from hard-to-reach crevices along the gum line.

Signs it is time for a new brush

If you notice frayed bristle tips, fading indicator bristles, or have recently recovered from a seasonal cold or flu, you should swap your brush immediately. Keeping clean bristles prevents bacterial accumulation and protects delicate enamel. Learn more about our preventative cleaning guidelines or schedule your semi-annual inspection.
